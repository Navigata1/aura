# T54 training-data provenance review: SwiftF0 and Basic Pitch, 2026-11-08

Every reading here is an engineering interpretation of license and dataset terms, not legal advice. It is not an owner
act, a license acceptance or a judgment of quality. No weights, datasets or archives were downloaded: only
documentation, code and terms pages were read.

The two JSON records are authoritative, and both verdicts are **fail**:

| `prv_basic-pitch-0.4.0` ([basic-pitch-0.4.0.json](basic-pitch-0.4.0.json)) | `basic-pitch` | fail | individual, research; `permissive_label_nc_data`, `unverified_training_data` |

## Question, rule and method

**Question.** May the studio run the component in the llc lane, judged by the terms of every input its shipped model
was trained on?

**Rule** (ADR-0011 rules 5 to 7, `contracts/registry/lanes.v1.json`). A review passes only when every training input
is identified and its terms, found in a primary source, permit commercial use. An input whose terms cannot be found
fails the review as unverifiable. A pass widens the row to llc and removes its training-data blocker. A fail keeps
the row in individual and research, with `permissive_label_nc_data` when a checked input bars commercial use and
`unverified_training_data` when an input could not be identified. `tests/foundation/test_provenance_reviews.py`
derives the verdict and those classes from each record, and fails when the register row disagrees or a record cites
an unhashed source.

**Method.**
1. Identify the model file the package ships and link it to the run that trained it.
2. Read that run's inputs from primary sources: the paper, the README or dataset card at a pinned revision, and the
   training code and configuration.
3. Read each input's terms from its own primary source.

Every source was fetched once, on 2026-11-08 between 02:37 and 02:50 UTC. Its URL, size and SHA-256 are in the
record, and every hash was re-checked against the kept copy. The copies stay on the studio volume under
`evidence/T54`, not in this repository.

## SwiftF0 0.3.0: fail

**Model.** The wheel's `swift_f0/model.onnx` (git blob `3619f9ab`) is byte-identical to `runs/seed1/model.onnx` in
`lars76/swift-f0-training` at `a3095d2` ("Release 0.3.0"), whose README calls `runs/seed1` the shipped model. The
paper (arXiv 2508.18440v1) describes SwiftF0 0.1.x, and its training mix (NSynth, PTDB-TUG, MIR-1K, MDB-stem-synth,
SpeechSynth, CHiME-Home noise) is not this model's. `config.py` trains on five corpora from the `train.tar` split of
`lars1234/pitch-benchmark` (revision `e98642f`). The augmentation pools come from that split, as its dataset card and
the preparation code at `6f21369` (`build_train`, `POOLS`, `ROOMS`) show.

| Training input | Role | Terms as read | Commercial use |
|---|---|---|---|
| pitch-benchmark train split | the compilation | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, research use; no rights beyond its sources' | not permitted |
| MDB-stem-synth 1.0.0 | corpus, weight 0.35 | CC BY-NC 4.0 | not permitted |
| NSynth (train split, acoustic, 1046.5 to 2093.75 Hz) | corpus, weight 0.15 | CC BY 4.0 | permitted |
| PTDB-TUG | corpus, weight 0.1667 | ODbL 1.0, contents DbCL 1.0 | permitted |
| MOCHA-TIMIT (8 speakers) | corpus, weight 0.1667 | research, educational and individual use only | not permitted |
| CMU ARCTIC (bdl, jmk, slt) | corpus, weight 0.1667 | any purpose, commercial or otherwise | permitted |
| LibriSpeech train-clean-100 | speech pool | CC BY 4.0 | permitted |
| MUSDB18 (104 tracks, no vocals) | music pool | educational purposes only | not permitted |
| TAU Urban Acoustic Scenes 2019 | scene pool | experimental and non-commercial only | not permitted |
| RIRS_NOISES simulated impulse responses | room pool | Apache 2.0 | permitted |
| coloured noise | generated by `augment.py` | no third-party data | permitted |

**Not training data.** The `eval.tar` corpora and their scene and room sources, which `build_eval` renders and
`build_train` never reads, and the paper's 0.1.x mix.

**Why it fails.** The compilation is non-commercial, and four of its nine third-party sources bar commercial use on
their own terms. The TAU licence counts distributing results achieved by use of the work as commercial use. The
laryngograph corpora's labels are a consensus of three classical estimators run on the laryngograph signal (Praat,
Harvest and an EGG peak picker), so no third-party model's output entered the labels.

## Basic Pitch 0.4.0: fail

**Model.** `basic_pitch/saved_models/icassp_2022`. The TensorFlow SavedModel has one commit, the initial public commit
`eea661f` of 2022-05-12, the day arXiv v2 of the paper was posted. The ONNX, TFLite and CoreML files were added on
2024-02-27 as serialized copies of it. The README's note that the library "improved" beyond the paper therefore does
not point to a retrained model. Table 1 of arXiv 2203.09893v2 lists the training sets, and the v0.4.0
`DATASET_SAMPLING_FREQUENCY` samples the same five.

| Training input | Role | Terms as read | Commercial use |
|---|---|---|---|
| GuitarSet 1.1.0 | 648 tracks, weight 2 | CC BY 4.0 | permitted |
| MAESTRO v2.0.0 | 1,154 performances, weight 5 | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 | not permitted |
| Slakh2100-redux | 1,590 stems, weight 2 | CC BY 4.0 | permitted |
| iKala | 252 excerpts, weight 2 | end-user agreement: academic purposes only, no profit | not permitted |
| MedleyDB pitch-tracking subset | 103 stems, weight 2 | non-commercial research only (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 link) | not permitted |
| noise and reverb augmentation sources | unidentified | not identified anywhere | unverifiable |

**Not training data.** Molina and Phenicx, which are test sets only.

**Why it fails.** Three of the five datasets bar commercial use. The paper adds noise, equalization filters and reverb
during training but names no source for them, and none of the 24 Python modules of the v0.4.0 package applies an
augmentation. That input alone would also fail the review as unverifiable. The row therefore gains
`permissive_label_nc_data` and keeps `unverified_training_data`.

## The reading behind both verdicts

Whether a trained model is adapted material of its training data, or a result achieved by using it, is legally
unsettled. ADR-0011 resolves that doubt toward the narrower lanes: a permissive model label over non-commercial
training data is not eligible for llc. Either row could widen only after a new review. That review needs either a
written commercial permission from every blocking rights holder (an owner-recorded `license-acceptance/1` covering it)
or a model retrained on inputs that all permit commercial use.

## Limits and what stays open

- **Not `evidence/1`.** These records are not `evidence/1` records. That schema has no check kind or subject type for
  a training-data review and no field for its datasets, and `docs/evidence` holds exports of the store. Making them
  store evidence records needs a contract change, which T95's Kim Mel-Band-RoFormer check would share.
- **SwiftF0 limits.** The contents of `train.tar` are read from its configuration, card and preparation code. The
  training log records no archive hash. The CMU ARCTIC licence was read in the unpacked voice directories, and
  festvox.org serves no HTTPS.
- **Stale wording elsewhere.** The engine-profile notes in `contracts/registry/engine-profiles.v1.json` (copied into
  three invalid fixtures) still say "until the provenance review". So do the spec, architecture and ADR-0011 lane
  tables. The verdicts leave every lane as those texts state it.
- **Basic Pitch's NOTICE.** The `apache_notice` obligation stays deferred to the contract-fix PR that T03 named.
