# Licensing doctrine: every component and artifact carries its terms, every project has an operator lane, and AURA enforces it

Status: accepted (2026-10-09). Owner decision O-06 (lane per project, default individual; Sabbath Echo and every IDC
venture are `llc`). From D-11. AURA's own code license is **proposed** and owner-pending (Q5): all rights reserved
until Jon chooses MIT or Apache-2.0 before the repo goes public. Release plans are owner-pending (Q4); the default is
personal, with an AI-disclosure-ready manifest on every export.

A permissive code license does not clear the weights (dozens of cases in §4). YuE2's individual-creator permission
excludes company use. Demucs weights are research and personal only by their author's statement. SwiftF0 and CREPE are
permissive labels over non-commercial training data. MiniMax and Stability carry obligations.

**Decision.**

1. **Component register.** Code, weights and output terms, gating, obligations, redistribution status, allowed lanes, a
   process-boundary flag and verification status, for every component. `contracts/registry/components.v1.json` is
   regenerated byte-for-byte from `docs/research/evidence-register.json`, and CI checks it.
2. **AURA's own code** takes a permissive license (Q5). Every component must already pass the open-source-readiness
   register, so going public later is a license choice, not a cleanup.
3. **Never vendor or redistribute weights.** They download at runtime from a pinned source (a Hugging Face repo and
   revision, a pinned URL with sha256, or a file inside a pinned package), after Jon accepts the license as an owner
   act, before any download, gated or not, recorded as `license-acceptance/1` under `models/licenses/`. CI rejects weight files by size and
   extension.
4. **Copyleft and non-commercial components run only as optional separate processes behind the worker protocol**: GPL
   Matchering (v2), LGPL PESTO if adopted, and non-commercial weights. AGPL projects (ACE-Step-DAW, openDAW,
   VoiceStudio, Ruach Studio) are never vendored or copied, and JUCE is used only under its EULA. CI runs a license
   allowlist over the control environment and the web bundle: MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, ISC, 0BSD, PSF, CC0 and OFL (fonts);
   UNKNOWN or NOASSERTION fails unless a register row names it; LGPL, GPL, AGPL, NC, CC-BY-SA, SSPL and BUSL are
   denied.
5. **Operator lanes**: `individual` (the default; Jon personally), `llc` (Island Development Crew ventures, Sabbath Echo
   included) and `research` (private experiments, never released). Non-commercial and research-only components are
   allowed only in individual and research. Every artifact carries `operator_lane` and `eligible_lanes`, enforced at
   seven points (ADR-0029).
6. **Lanes of the v1 components**: pYIN+HMM, NeutralVoice and ACE in all lanes; SwiftF0 in individual and research
   until its provenance review; Demucs and demucs-mlx in individual and research; YuE2 in individual and research;
   HeartMuLa and MiniMax (P6) eligible for LLC, with MiniMax's AUP #11 disclosure carried into the manifest.
7. **Release-blocker list** (RECONCILIATION §6 D-11 item 6, kept current in the register):
   - non-commercial weights: YuE2 and its GGUF conversions, SheetSage2/MERT2, MuQ and MuQ-MuLan, SongEval, MuLaCover,
     OmniVoice, F5-TTS, the DiffSinger vocoders, Shao, MusicGen/JASCO, SOME/GAME, MuScriptor, the HumTrans dataset;
   - non-commercial by author statement: Demucs weights and the demucs-mlx conversions;
   - research-only or academic: SongBench, SongPrep, Fish, VibeVoice;
   - unknown license: BS-RoFormer SW, LeVo, SCNet weights, ROSVOT weights, RMVPE community weights;
   - permissive label over non-commercial data: SwiftF0, CREPE and torchcrepe;
   - contested: RVC and rmvpe weights, heartlib-mlx, whether YuE2's permission covers GGUF conversions;
   - copyleft: Rubber Band, espeak-ng (through Kokoro or any espeak-backed G2P), PESTO, a GPL ffmpeg build;
   - unverified until checked: mir_eval, mido, the G2P, the MIDI synth and soundfont, the audio decoder, and the stack
     libraries (FastAPI, uv, React, Vite, the codegen tools);
   - obligations: MiniMax (UI naming, revenue threshold, AUP) and Stability (under US$1M, registration, attribution,
     no training on outputs).
8. **Suno output** has its own rules (ADR-0021, ADR-0036).

**Why.** O-06; C9, C35, GAP-05, GAP-06. Lanes turn "who is this work for?" into a check that can fail before anything
loads.

## Consequences

A YuE2 or Demucs artifact can never enter an LLC project, and moving an individual project that holds one to `llc` is
refused. Whether a single-member LLC counts as a "company" under YuE2's permission is publicly unanswered, so LLC work
never uses YuE2 unless its licensors grant a commercial license in writing. These readings are interpretations, not
legal advice.

**Amended in the 2026-10-09 design review** (architecture.md, "Design review log"). individual and llc forbid a component whose license is still UNVERIFIED, so such rows are research-only until their license ticket verifies them; llc also forbids `unverified_training_data`, which narrows Kim Mel-Band-RoFormer and Basic Pitch to individual and research until their provenance reviews. Non-commercial scorers are individual and research (O-06). An export carries its closure's release blockers, and is release-clear only in a releasable lane with each blocker covered by an explicit permission recorded in a license acceptance.

**Reverse if:** Jon decides AURA stays private forever; rule 4 then relaxes to record-keeping. Rule 3 never relaxes:
weights are never committed or vendored, even to a private repository. A component's lanes
change only when its license verdict changes in the register.
